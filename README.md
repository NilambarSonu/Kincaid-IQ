## What Is Kincaid IQ?

Kincaid IQ is an enterprise SaaS platform purpose-built for the **self-funded employer health benefits industry** — the largest under-audited pool of fiduciary capital in the American economy. It replaces the fragmented ecosystem of spreadsheets, static benchmarks, and opaque vendor relationships with a single, evidence-grade intelligence engine that makes every dollar traceable, every claim auditable, and every fiduciary decision defensible.

The platform serves **fiduciary advisors, benefit consultants, employer plan sponsors, brokers, and TPAs** who manage self-funded healthcare plans under ERISA. It is not a dashboard that shows numbers — it is an **evidence engine that shows receipts**.

---

## The Problem We Solve

Every year, mid-market self-funded employers route **tens of millions of dollars** through a chain of agents — brokers, third-party administrators (TPAs), pharmacy benefit managers (PBMs), and carriers — under a legal regime (ERISA) that names the *employer* as the fiduciary of record and holds it personally accountable for the prudence of every dollar spent.

Yet the employer is structurally the **least-informed party** in its own value chain:

- **PBMs** retain rebates and apply opaque spread pricing that can cost plans 30–40% more than market benchmarks
- **Brokers** receive commissions tied to plan spend — creating incentives that are *structurally opposed* to cost reduction
- **TPAs** operate under contracts with audit-restriction clauses that prevent fiduciaries from seeing their own data
- **Carriers** negotiate in-network rates that are invisible to the plan sponsor, with no mechanism for comparison

The result is **systematic EBITDA leakage** — millions of dollars leaving the enterprise every year without consideration, presenting on the income statement as recurring cost rather than as the recoverable liability it actually is.

**Traditional benefits software documents this exposure. Kincaid IQ resolves it.**

## The Constitutional Doctrine

Every assertion Kincaid IQ makes is governed by a constitutional doctrine that ensures fiduciary-grade credibility:

**Four Hard Rules:**
1. One screen, four tiles, always — executive cognition is bounded
2. Every number is clickable into receipts — drill, not tooltip
3. No export without a SHA-256 evidence manifest — portability of proof
4. Narratives never outrun evidence — `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` is a valid, first-class finding

**Three Confidence Tiers:**
- **CERTIFIED** — Contract clause + claim record + remittance, reconciled
- **MODELED** — Inference from incomplete evidence, with bounds disclosed
- **INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE** — No finding issued; logged as a gap

There is no fourth tier. The discipline is in the refusal to invent one.
