You’re describing something ambitious—but if you don’t structure the logic behind the clicks, it turns into a gimmicky dashboard fast. The power here isn’t “4 tiles × 9 clicks”… it’s progressive narrative compression → expansion for the C-suite.

Let’s design this like a system Claude can actually build—not just a concept.

⸻

🧠 Kincaid IQ War Room UI

“4-Tile Recursive Drill-Down System” (C-Suite Narrative Engine)

Core Principle

Each click must do ONE thing:

Convert ambiguity → decision-grade clarity

Not “more data.”
Different data, at a higher resolution and different abstraction layer.

⸻

🧩 LEVEL 0 — WAR ROOM (ENTRY STATE)

4 Tiles (Always Fixed Categories)
	1.	Financial Exposure
	2.	Contract Integrity
	3.	Operational Behavior
	4.	Strategic Leverage

Each tile shows:
	•	1 dominant KPI (large)
	•	1 anomaly signal
	•	1 directional indicator (↑ ↓ →)
	•	1 sentence narrative

👉 Example:

“$12.4M leakage identified — driven by spread + rebate opacity”

⸻

🔁 CLICK ARCHITECTURE (9 LEVELS DEEP)

Each click = change in narrative layer

⸻

🔵 LEVEL 1 — SIGNAL DECOMPOSITION

“What is driving this?”

Break tile into 3–5 drivers:
	•	Spread Pricing
	•	Rebate Misalignment
	•	Specialty Inflation
	•	Formulary Drift

Each becomes a mini-tile.

⸻

🔵 LEVEL 2 — MECHANISM VIEW

“How does it actually happen?”

Now we shift from categories → mechanics

Example:
	•	Ingredient Cost vs Plan Paid
	•	MAC List Manipulation
	•	Rebate Retention Timing

👉 This is where most dashboards fail—they never cross into mechanism layer

⸻

🔵 LEVEL 3 — CONTRACT MAPPING

“Where is this allowed?”

Map each mechanism to:
	•	Clause
	•	Absence of clause
	•	Ambiguous language

Output:
	•	“This behavior exists because §7.3 does not define pass-through timing”

⸻

🔵 LEVEL 4 — CLAIMS TRACE (REALITY LAYER)

“Show me proof”

Now we anchor in:
	•	Actual claim examples
	•	NDC-level detail
	•	Pharmacy-level variance

👉 This is your “Walgreens moment” layer

⸻

🔵 LEVEL 5 — PATTERN DETECTION

“Is this isolated or systemic?”
	•	Frequency analysis
	•	Cohort clustering
	•	Time-based patterns

Output:
	•	“Occurs in 78% of specialty claims”
	•	“Spikes at quarter-end”

⸻

🔵 LEVEL 6 — COUNTERFACTUAL MODEL

“What should have happened?”

Simulate:
	•	Transparent pricing model
	•	Alternative contract terms
	•	NADAC / pass-through baseline

Output:
	•	“Expected cost: $4.2M vs actual $6.1M”

⸻

🔵 LEVEL 7 — RISK & FIDUCIARY EXPOSURE

“What is the consequence?”

Translate into:
	•	ERISA breach risk
	•	Fiduciary exposure
	•	Audit vulnerability

Tone shift:
👉 Now speaking to GC + Board

⸻

🔵 LEVEL 8 — LEVERAGE POINTS

“What can we do about it?”

This is where your product becomes lethal.
	•	Contract rewrite targets
	•	RFP leverage triggers
	•	Vendor pressure points

Example:

“Force rebate pass-through at adjudication, not reconciliation”

⸻

🔴 LEVEL 9 — C-SUITE NARRATIVE VIEW (END STATE)

This is the payoff.

A single screen:
	•	3–5 bullet executive story
	•	Financial impact
	•	Risk statement
	•	Recommended action

👉 Example Output:

Narrative:
The PBM is extracting margin through spread pricing and delayed rebate pass-through, enabled by ambiguous contract language and lack of real-time audit rights.

Impact:
$18.6M annual leakage

Risk:
Potential ERISA fiduciary breach exposure

Action:
Immediate RFP with enforced pass-through + audit controls
