MVP MODULE 11

Form 5500 Intelligence Distribution & Opportunity Management System

Strategic Purpose

Most organizations treat Form 5500 filings as static compliance documents.

Sales Console OS™ treats Form 5500 filings as a continuously renewing market intelligence asset.

The objective is not merely to collect filings, but to transform every newly available Form 5500 into a prioritized opportunity, route it to the appropriate team, monitor engagement, and create measurable accountability throughout the organization.

⸻

New Filing Intelligence Pipeline

Daily Filing Monitoring

The platform continuously monitors newly available Form 5500 filings and updates.

When a new filing is detected, the system automatically:

1. Ingests filing data.
2. Normalizes employer information.
3. Calculates opportunity metrics.
4. Scores the account.
5. Identifies existing relationships.
6. Assigns ownership.
7. Distributes intelligence.
8. Tracks engagement outcomes.

No filing enters the system without accountability.
