SALES CONSOLE OS™

STANFORD MBA + MIT PhD MVP SPECIFICATION

Kincaid IQ Executive Intelligence Platform

Vision

Sales organizations traditionally operate on fragmented data, intuition, and reactive outreach. The Sales Console OS™ transforms sales execution into a data-driven intelligence discipline by unifying employer intelligence, Form 5500 data, consultant relationships, RFP activity, and AI-powered opportunity prioritization within a single executive command center.

The objective is simple:

Identify the highest-value opportunities, allocate resources intelligently, and create a compounding intelligence advantage that improves with every interaction.

⸻

MVP OBJECTIVE

Create a unified intelligence platform that allows SiriusB leadership, sales teams, and consultants to:

* Select a target market.
* Analyze employers within that market.
* Prioritize opportunities.
* Monitor RFP activity.
* Leverage Form 5500 intelligence.
* Execute targeted outreach.
* Track outcomes.
* Continuously improve decision-making.

The MVP must deliver meaningful business value immediately while establishing the foundation for future predictive capabilities.
