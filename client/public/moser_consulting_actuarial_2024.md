### 8.4 Laser Risk Analysis

**Laser Definition:** A carrier-imposed individual deductible higher than the contract ISD applied to a specifically identified member, eliminating or reducing stop-loss recovery on that individual.

**Laser Risk Formula:**

$$L_{exposure} = \sum_{i \in Lasered} C_i - ISD_{laser,i}$$

**Assumption A9:** If 3 of 9 high-cost members are lasered at renewal (industry behavior for members with chronic specialty drug spend), laser exposure equals:

$$L_{exposure} = 3 \times (\bar{C}_{HC} - ISD_{laser})$$

**At $\bar{C}_{HC}$ = $527,000 (midpoint), $ISD_{laser}$ = $500,000:**

$$L_{exposure} = 3 \times (\$527,000 - \$500,000) = \mathbf{\$81,000}$$

**At $\bar{C}_{HC}$ = $527,000, $ISD_{laser}$ = $1,000,000 (full laser — common for high-cost specialty):**

$$L_{exposure} = 3 \times \$527,000 = \mathbf{\$1,581,000 \text{ unrecovered}$$

> 🔴 **CRITICAL RISK:** Full laser on 3 of 9 specialty members = $1.58M in plan liability previously assumed covered by stop-loss. This is a material balance sheet exposure that may not appear in budget models.
